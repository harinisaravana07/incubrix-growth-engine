## 1. Market Insight & Priority ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

### Priority Segment
- **Target Segment:** Professional Creator Studios & High-Volume B2B/EdTech Creators (50k–1M audience).
- **Target Decision-Makers:** Creator Founders, Head of Content/Production, Content Operations Leads.
- **Current Stack Pain Point ("The Tool Sprawl Tax"):**
  - **Ideation:** Notion + ChatGPT / Claude
  - **Creation:** Google Docs + Loom + Premiere Pro / Descript
  - **Repurposing:** OpusClip + Canva + manual copywriters
  - **Publishing:** Buffer / Hypefury / Native YouTube & LinkedIn schedulers
  - **Analytics:** Native studio dashboards + Spreadsheets
  - **Total Cost:** 15–25 manual handoff hours per week wasted stitching disconnected tools together.

### Buying Triggers
1. **The Repurposing Deficit:** High production of 30–60 min long-form videos with low or erratic short-form/multi-channel distribution.
2. **Team Handoff Latency:** Editors, thumbnail designers, and writers blocked waiting on creator approvals across Slack/WhatsApp/Email.
3. **Analytics Blindspot:** Inability to correlate ideation themes with downstream retention and revenue across all platforms.

## 3. Discovery Playbook: The 5-Stage Conversation Framework

Mapped directly to IncuBrix’s five stages: **Discover ➔ Create ➔ Repurpose ➔ Publish ➔ Learn**.

### 5 Probing Discovery Questions
1. **Discover:** *"When you finish researching a topic, how does that research carry over into your recording script—or do you have to copy-paste between 3 different documents?"*
2. **Create:** *"Where in your production flow do drafts stall the longest between you and your editors?"*
3. **Repurpose:** *"You drop a 45-minute YouTube video every Tuesday. How many days pass before that single video becomes 5 short clips, a LinkedIn carousel, and a newsletter?"*
4. **Publish:** *"How many browser tabs does your team open on publishing day just to schedule the same release across platforms?"*
5. **Learn:** *"When a video outperforms expectations, can you trace back which hook in the ideation phase caused the spike, or are you looking at isolated analytics in YouTube Studio?"*
